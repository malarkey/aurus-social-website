 <p class="alt-lede">Aurus is a purpose-led business operating across Britain and Ireland, helping to address the shortfall in good-quality social housing while offering investors a rewarding, share-based model built around income security and long-term performance.</p>

 <p>At the heart of our work is the recognition that the UK is facing a significant and ongoing housing shortage. Reports suggest that around 1.3 million households are on waiting lists for social housing, while well over 150,000 children are living in temporary accommodation. This crisis is costing local authorities close to £2 billion every year, and responding effectively will require an estimated 2 million new social and affordable homes.</p>

 <p>As a specialist in social housing, we are proud to be playing an important role in meeting this need, while helping to secure a more sustainable future for the development of social housing.</p>

 <p>We’re doing that by making it a more attractive, reliable, and predictable asset class. Our share-based alternative to conventional buy-to-let investment offers fixed, regular returns without the risks of owning or managing properties.</p>



aside: |
 <h2 data-size="l">Focus on supported housing</h2>

 <p>For many years, Britain has suffered from a shortage of good-quality housing. Too much of the country’s housing stock is old, poorly insulated, and prone to condensation, damp, and mould.</p>

 <p>Housebuilding rates vary, but population growth and demographic change have created persistent under-supply. Only a fraction of new housing is genuinely affordable, and most is aimed at homeowners rather than renters. Many smaller landlords have left the sector over the past decade, restricting supply, increasing competition, and contributing to rising rents.</p>
