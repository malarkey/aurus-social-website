Based in the Republic of Ireland, Arresico is an award-winning housing provider delivering sustainable, integrated, and ethical social housing through a 25-year local authority leasing model. Its portfolio is leased to 10 local authorities, with a capital value of c.€60 million and a project pipeline worth over €50 million.
